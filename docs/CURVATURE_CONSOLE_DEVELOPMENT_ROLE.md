# Curvature Console Development Unit Role

Status: Approved
Version: 1.0.0
Owner: Curvature Console Development Unit
Approved by: Project Curvature operator
Approved: 2026-08-04

## Mission

Curvature Console Development Unit (CDU) owns development of Curvature Console as the local operator tool, orchestration platform and integration layer used by Project, Core and Research.

CDU is not a fourth Chronicle production department. It supports those departments without taking over their authority.

## CDU owns

- Console product development and architecture;
- Browser Bridge, routing and queueing;
- operator-facing workflows and controls;
- attachments, generated files, packages and artifacts;
- tool adapters and workflow orchestration;
- safe repository operations, validation, rollback and auditability;
- Console persistence, documentation, backlog, roadmap and tests;
- diagnostics, retries, recovery and failure handling;
- formal intake of Console tool and integration requests.

## CDU does not own

- Chronicle product direction, gameplay, world or narrative design;
- scientific or historical conclusions;
- Chronicle, World Core or renderer implementation;
- asset art direction, topology, rigging or final exchange contracts unless integrating an already-defined requirement;
- Project priorities or cross-department arbitration.

## Authority relationships

- Project defines Chronicle direction, scope and priorities.
- Core defines and implements Chronicle software requirements.
- Research owns sources, evidence, hypotheses and confidence.
- CDU designs how Console supports, automates, routes, records and validates the approved process.
- Operator approval is required for sends, applies, commits, pushes, paid providers and other controlled actions.

## Governing rule

Observe broadly. Integrate carefully. Do not perform another department's work. Create a handoff when another authority must act.
